# Synthetic ability bank — verification and limitations

The 10,000,000-row synthetic adapter checkpoint was independently copied and verified at `training/abilitybank` in the v1.0 training host. It is intentionally excluded from the lightweight v1.0 source distribution. In an offline full-checkpoint audit on 2026-11-23, **all 200 row-batch SHA-256 digests** matched the stored FP16 weights and the status bitmap recorded 10,000,000 synthetic-trained rows. Each row contains 32 individually updated scalar weights. The targets are generated by deterministic synthetic teachers; no market data, real software repository tasks, general intelligence benchmarks or external environment rewards were used.

**Not established:** 10 million semantically distinct skills, 10 million full neural models, trading edge, transfer learning, real-world mastery, or trustworthy autonomy. A digest proves internal data consistency, not authentic data provenance. Checkpoint file byte hashes and schema are in the original training certification JSON and must be revalidated before reuse.

### Per-agent 411,000-weight pilot

An additional 100 separate neural agent policies were initialized and trained by actual PyTorch backpropagation: five AdamW steps each, totaling 500 optimizer steps. The pilot uses 32-dimensional synthetic feature vectors to regress an eight-action teacher. Each agent has 411,000 FP16-stored model parameters and its ledger stores SHA-256 integrity hashes. A full 100/100 weight-row check passed. Aggregate mean training loss fell from 1.3569 to 0.3524; average held-out post-training loss was 0.7155. **There is no independently proven general intelligence, profitable trading, or mastery.**

The pilot bank requires 82,200,000 bytes, and the 10,000-agent production bank would require 8,220,000,000 bytes just for agent weights. The pilot uses its own 100-agent bank and is NOT automatically deployable as a full 10,000-agent fleet. The source distribution excludes the pilot weights to keep the release small; see `training/AGENT_PILOT_100_RESULTS.jsonl` and `training/AGENT_PILOT_100_VERIFICATION.json`. Reproduce with the included `training/individual_weight_bank.py`, after checking your RAM/disk budgets.
